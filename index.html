Feature Engineering Impact

Domain-specific transformations created in this project.

1. ZeroBalance Flag

36.2% of all customers hold exactly a $0 balance. Rather than letting standard scaling treat 0 as a continuous midpoint, this binary flag allows the model to cleanly isolate fee-sensitive zero-balance debit accounts.

2. Balance-to-Salary Ratio

Calculated as Balance / EstimatedSalary. Reveals financial capital density relative to earnings. Wealthy savers in Germany with ratios exceeding 1.5x churn at elevated rates due to competitive interest rate hunting.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Customer Churn Machine Learning Findings & Strategic Roadmap

Analysis of 10,000 retail bank customers evaluated across Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting algorithms.

🏆 1. Model Champion Recommendation

Deploy the Gradient Boosting Classifier tuned to a 0.30 decision threshold. At this operational threshold:

  • Catches 65.1% of all churners (Recall) compared to only 48.6% at the default 0.50 threshold.
  • Maintains a strong 62.5% Precision (out of every 100 flagged customers, 62 genuinely depart).
  • Achieves top-in-class 0.872 Test ROC-AUC and 0.725 PR-AUC.
  • Outperforms Logistic Regression by +0.095 AUC due to non-linear interaction mastery.

⚠️ 2. The 4 Critical Churn Drivers

#1
Customer Age:

Churn accelerates from 7.5% under age 30 up to 56.2% for ages 50–60. Middle-aged and pre-retirement customers constitute the highest loss risk.

#2
Product Count Paradox:

Holding 2 products is the safest state (7.6% churn). However, 3 products skyrockets churn to 82.7%, and 4 products hits 100%. Over-bundling creates severe friction.

#3
German Branch Exposure:

Germany accounts churn at 32.4%—exactly double the rates of France (16.2%) and Spain (16.7%), signaling branch/market-specific competitive vulnerabilities.

#4
Member Inactivity:

Inactive members churn at 26.9% vs 14.3% for active digital users. Engagement acts as a natural retention anchor.

🎯 3. Actionable Retention Strategy

Phase 1
Product Rationalization Campaign:

Review customer portfolios holding 3+ products. Simplify cross-sold products, consolidate statements, and remove hidden product fees.

Phase 2
Germany Market Retention Taskforce:

Benchmark German branch rates against local fintech rivals. Offer competitive deposit yield matching for German accounts holding >$80k.

Phase 3
Pre-Retirement (45-60) Wealth Advisory:

Deploy dedicated financial advisors to engage high-balance 45–60 year old clients with retirement planning before funds are transferred out.
